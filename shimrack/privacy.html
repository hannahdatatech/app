Shimrack privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

Shimrack collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your machine names, valve layouts, clearance specs, shim settings, the shims in your rack, and your valve checks (date, optional engine hours and note, measured clearances, shim sizes). They are saved in one file inside the app on your iPhone, protected by iOS file encryption, and included in your normal iPhone backup.

What the app does not do

No account or sign-in. No network connections: the app contains no code that sends anything over the internet. No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting tools, and no third-party software of any kind. No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

Sharing

If you tap Share list and plan, the app hands a text version of your shopping list and swap plan to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice and is handled by the app you pick.

Deleting your data

Delete checks or machines inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact
