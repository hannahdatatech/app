Common questions

How do I enter a ball? Tap the pins still standing, then tap Record. With nothing tapped, the button records a strike (first ball) or a spare.

I tapped the wrong pins. Use the undo arrow at the top right to take back the last ball. On a finished game, use the ••• menu → Reopen last ball.

What counts as a split? The USBC definition: the head pin is down and there's a gap between the pins left standing (for example 7-10, 3-10, 5-6). Splits are circled in gold on the scoresheet.

How does the spare drill work? Spare Sheet picks the leaves you miss most in real games. At practice, throw at a full rack along your spare line for that leave, then tap Made if all the target pins fell, or Missed.

What are League, Practice and Open play? Game types. Tap the chip on the game screen to change one, and filter the spare report by type. New games use the type of your last game.

Where are my games stored? Only on your iPhone. They're included in your normal device backup. There is no account and no sync.

Is there a game limit or subscription? No. You pay once and get unlimited games.
