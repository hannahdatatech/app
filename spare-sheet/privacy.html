Privacy Policy

Spare Sheet · effective November 12, 2026

Summary

Spare Sheet does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any information about you. It has no networking code, no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.

Data on your device

The games you enter (the pins standing after each ball, the game type, and the date of each game) and your spare-drill results are saved in a file inside the app on your iPhone, protected by iOS file protection. They never leave your device except as part of your own iCloud or computer backup, which Apple's privacy policy covers. Deleting a game, or the app, removes that data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
