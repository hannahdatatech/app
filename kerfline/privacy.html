Kerfline: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Kerfline ("the app") is a sheet-goods cut planner for iPhone, published by an independent developer.

No data is collected

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs. The app contains no networking code and does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

The cut list and sheet settings you enter are saved only in the app's private storage on your iPhone so they are there next time you open it. The developer never receives this data. Like other app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, which is managed by you and Apple. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Sharing

If you tap Share cut plan, the app creates an image of your cut plan and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (for example Messages, Mail, Files, Photos, or a printer) is chosen by you and governed by that app's privacy policy. If you choose "Save Image", iOS asks for permission to add the image to your photo library; the app cannot read your photos.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
