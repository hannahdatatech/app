Kerfline: Sheet Cut Planner

Kerfline lays out your cut list on plywood and other sheet goods using straight, saw-ready cuts, so you buy fewer sheets. It works offline, needs no account, and collects no data.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature ideas: email hannahdatatech@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Quick help

  • Entering fractions: type the whole inches on the number pad, then tap a fraction key (1/8 to 7/8). Tap 16ths for sixteenths. Decimals like 23.5 also work.
  • Kerf: the width of material your blade removes, usually 1/8" (3.2 mm). Every cut in the layout leaves this gap.
  • Grain lock: a locked part keeps its length running along the sheet length. Unlocked parts may be rotated to save material.
  • "Too large for this sheet": the part can't fit the stock size even when rotated. Check its size or pick a larger sheet.
  • Edit or delete a part: tap it to edit; swipe left to delete.
  • Share: after optimizing, tap the share button on the Sheets tab to send, print, or save the whole plan as one image.
