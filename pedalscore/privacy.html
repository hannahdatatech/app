Pedalscore privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

Pedalscore collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your class names and their segments: song titles and artists you type, lengths, RPM ranges, effort numbers, positions and cues. They are saved in one file inside the app on your iPhone, protected by iOS file encryption, and included in your normal iPhone backup.

What the app does not do

No account or sign-in. No network connections: the app contains no code that sends anything over the internet. No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting tools, and no third-party software of any kind. No access to your music library, location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.

Sharing and importing

If you share a class file or a PDF cue card, the app hands that file to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice and is handled by the app you pick. If you open a class file, the app reads only that file and adds the class to your library.

Deleting your data

Delete classes inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact
