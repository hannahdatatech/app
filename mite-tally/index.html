Questions

How do I log a count?
Open a hive, tap Count mites, pick the method and the number of bees in your sample, then tap the big hexagon once for each mite (or tap Type number). Tap Save.
How is "mites per 100 bees" worked out?
Mites counted ÷ bees in the sample × 100. A half-cup sample is roughly 300 bees, which is the starting value.
Where do the watch and action levels come from?
They start at 2 and 3 mites per 100 bees, a common US rule of thumb for the active season. Many guides use lower levels in spring. Set your own under the menu (top left) → Mite levels & reminders, following your local extension service or bee club. Mite Tally doesn't give treatment advice.
What does "Due" mean?
The hive hasn't been counted within the number of days you set (21 to start). Set it to Off for winter.
How do I record a treatment, split or new queen?
On the hive screen, tap Add note. Notes appear on the trend chart as a dotted line.
How do I delete a count?
Swipe left on it in the hive's history. To delete a whole hive, use the ••• menu on the hive screen.
Can I get my data out?
Yes: menu (top left on the home screen) → Export counts (CSV), then save or send the file.
Is my data backed up?
Your counts are stored only on your iPhone and are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). There is no account and no sync.
