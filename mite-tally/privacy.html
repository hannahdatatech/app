Mite Tally privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026

The short version

Mite Tally collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your hive names, mite counts (date, method, sample size, mites counted), notes, and your level settings. They are saved in a file inside the app on your iPhone, protected by iOS file encryption. They are included in your normal iPhone backup.

What the app does not do

No account or sign-in. No network connections: the app contains no code that sends anything over the internet. No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting tools, and no third-party software of any kind. No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

Sharing

If you choose Export counts (CSV), the app creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice and is handled by the app you pick.

Deleting your data

Delete counts, notes or hives inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact
